Privacy policy

OAF and your data

Last updated 26 October 2026

In short: OAF collects nothing. Your files are opened and edited on your phone, there are no accounts, ads or analytics, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you ask for it.

What OAF collects

Nothing. OAF has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

Your files

Files are opened, edited and saved on your phone. Recents, favourites and settings are kept in the app’s private storage and never leave the device.

When OAF goes online

  • Playing a video or audio link another app sends to OAF: the stream comes straight from that address.
  • Network drives you add (SMB, SFTP, FTP, WebDAV): OAF connects only to the servers you enter. Passwords are encrypted with a key held by Android’s keystore.
  • Removing a picture’s background or copying text out of a picture uses Google’s ML Kit through Google Play services, which runs on the phone; the picture isn’t uploaded. Play services may fetch the tool once, the first time.

Sharing over Wi-Fi

If you turn on Share over Wi-Fi, OAF runs a file server (FTP) on your phone so computers and other devices on the same network can open the folder you choose, and change it unless you pick read-only. It runs only until you stop it, with a notification the whole time. By default a user name and password are needed; that password is encrypted with a key held by Android’s keystore. FTP isn’t encrypted, so anyone watching the same network could see the files and password: share on networks you trust. OAF keeps a short list of recent activity (who signed in, what was copied) on the phone only.

Sharing

Files only leave the phone when you share them, send them to a picker that asked for them, upload them to your own server, or let a device on your network open them with Share over Wi-Fi.

Permissions

What OAF asks Android for, and why:

PermissionWhy
All files accessTo browse, open, copy, move and delete files anywhere on the phone, as a file manager does.
Query installed appsFor the Apps screen, which lists installed apps so you can share or back them up.
Install and remove appsTo install APK files you open and uninstall apps you choose. Android asks you each time.
Internet, network & Wi-Fi stateOnly for video links, network drives you add and Share over Wi-Fi, as above.
Foreground service & keep awakeSo Share over Wi-Fi keeps going with the screen off, with a notification and a Stop button.
Notifications & media playbackSo music keeps playing with the screen off, with controls in the notification.

Children

OAF isn't aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here and in the app's source repository, where every change can be seen.
